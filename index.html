How Scoring Works

Lead scores are generated by a transparent, rules-based demo engine — not a trained AI model and not a measure of objective lead quality. Every lead's score can be inspected in its detail view under "Why this lead received this score."

Budget High budget → up to +25 · Medium → up to +15 · Low → up to +8
Timeline Immediate → +25 · Within 1 month → +18 · 3–6 months → +10 · No timeline → +2
Project Type Whole-home / kitchen / addition → up to +15 · Smaller projects → up to +8
Buying-Intent Language +5 per detected keyword (ready, book, quote, urgent, start, price, consultation...), capped at +20
